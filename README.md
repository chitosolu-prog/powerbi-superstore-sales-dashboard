# powerbi-superstore-sales-dashboard
Interactive Power BI dashboard analysing sales, profitability and regional performance using data visualization, DAX and business intelligence tools and techniques. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable insights, enabling stakeholders identify profit drivers, regional performance trends, and areas of improvement.

## Business Objectives
The dashboard was created to:
- Monitor the business performance.
- Support data driven decisons.
- Analyse profit distribution across states.
- Identify top performing product categories.

  ## KPI Cards
  Total Sales 2.30M
  Total Profit 286.40K
  Order 10K
  Products 2K

  ## Key Insights
  ### Positive Findings
  - Technology generated the highest profit.
  - Copiers, Phones, and Accessories were major profit drivers.
  - Observed strong profit performance in multiple state.

     ### Areas of Improvement

    - Table generated negative profit.
    - Opportunity exist to optimize discount strategies.
    - Furniture category showed lower profitability compared to technology.
   
      ## Business Recommendations

      - Review discount policies and limit to 20%.
      - Review pricing strategies for low-profit furniture products.
      - Promote Standard Class Shipping.
      - Increase inventory in technology category products.
     
        ## Tools Used

        - Power BI Desktop
        - Power Query
        - DAX
        - Data Modelling
        - Interactive Visualisations
